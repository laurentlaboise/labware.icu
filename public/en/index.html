FAQ

Questions before you badge in.

What is Labware?

Labware is a playable AI company where 68 named agent interfaces, coordinated through Alex, collaborate under human direction inside a digital Tower.

Why 64 of 68 colleagues?

The public registry publishes 68 named interfaces across 13 departments plus specialized and expansion layers. Customer plans open up to 64 of those colleagues on Agency and Enterprise. The other four remain on the published company map and are not extra paid seats.

Is Labware autonomous?

No. It is designed for human direction and review. Current workflow runs are simulated and approval-gated, not autonomous real-world execution.

How many agents are included?

The public registry names 68 colleague interfaces. The anonymous demo includes 3 roles. Free includes 8, Starter 16, Pro 33, and Agency or Enterprise 64 of those colleagues.

What counts as a message?

A message is a metered interaction sent through the product. Daily limits depend on the active plan and reset according to the application’s usage window.

What are artifacts and conferences?

Artifacts are saved structured outputs. Conferences bring several relevant roles into a shared, reviewable conversation and are available from Starter upward.

What are workflows?

They are structured workflow blueprints with defined steps and approval points. Current workflow execution is explicitly simulated and available with eligible plans.

Which AI providers are used?

Provider availability varies by deployment and configuration. Labware may use fallback behavior, so this website does not promise a particular provider or model.

Can AI output be wrong?

Yes. Review output before relying on it, especially for legal, financial, medical, employment, safety, or other consequential decisions.

How is account access protected?

The application uses signed HttpOnly sessions, exact-origin protections for authenticated mutations, and rate limits. These are safeguards, not a guarantee against every risk.

How is data processed?

Account and workspace data may be processed by configured hosting, storage, billing, and AI providers. Review the draft Privacy Policy and AI Disclosure for current details and unresolved items.

Can I change a paid plan?

New paid checkout starts inside the authenticated Tower. Existing paid customers may need to contact billing support for plan changes. Final cancellation and refund terms remain in draft.

Is Enterprise available?

Enterprise requirements are discussed directly. Custom usage or commercial terms are not active until confirmed in writing.

Which languages are supported?

This public website is available in English and Lao. The Lao edition is marked beta until native-language review is formally recorded.

Ready to meet your AI team?

Start with 8 colleagues and 50 messages a day. Upgrade when you need a larger team, conferences, or approval-gated workflow simulation.
